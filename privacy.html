Noon Bell: Western Duels — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Noon Bell does not collect, sell or share your personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Your progress stays on your own phone.

This policy explains what the Noon Bell: Western Duels app ("the game," "we") stores, the few things that go through Apple or Google, and your choices. It applies to the Noon Bell apps on the Apple App Store and Google Play.

What stays on your phone

The game saves your progress on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This includes:

  • Your bounty cash, Gold Nuggets, items, outfits and gunslingers
  • Game stats, such as duels won, head shots and fastest draw
  • Season progress, challenges and firing range bests
  • Settings, such as sound and volume
  • The name you type on your wanted poster

This information is never sent to us or to anyone else. The name you type is checked on your phone against a list of words that aren't allowed. That check happens on your device, and the name is not sent anywhere.

Purchases

Season passes are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play. Apple or Google handles the payment, and we never see your name, card number or billing details. They tell the game only which pass you own, so it can unlock your rewards and let you restore them on a new device.

Apple's and Google's own privacy policies cover the payment itself.

Leaderboards and fonts

Leaderboards. If you're signed in to Apple Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android), the game sends three scores to those services: your fastest draw, your biggest bounty cash-out and your best firing range score. Other players see these scores next to your Game Center or Play Games nickname. Apple and Google run these services under their own privacy policies. You can sign out of Game Center or Play Games in your phone's settings at any time, and the game still works without them.

Fonts. The game's Western lettering is loaded from Google Fonts. Like any website visit, this means Google receives your device's internet address when the fonts download. No game data is included.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-ups
  • No advertising or ad networks
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising IDs
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photos
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your information

Children

Noon Bell doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Purchases go through Apple's and Google's parental controls, such as Ask to Buy and Family Link.

Deleting your data

All game data lives on your phone. Deleting the app removes it. Season passes you bought stay with your Apple or Google account and can be restored with Restore purchases in Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example when friend challenges are added, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@noonbellgames.com.
