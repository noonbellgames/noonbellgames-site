Support

Need help with Noon Bell: Western Duels? Email us at support@noonbellgames.com and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

Common questions

I got a new phone. How do I get my season pass back?
Open the game, go to Settings and tap Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple or Google account you bought it with.

Where is my progress saved?
On your phone. Deleting the app removes your progress, so keep it installed to keep your bounty cash and gear.

How do I get on the leaderboards?
Sign in to Game Center (iPhone) or Google Play Games (Android). Your best scores are sent automatically.
